This guide explains how to use your Dia Shield participant dashboard in clear, everyday language.

Participant guide

For participants

Using your health dashboard

1

Sign in

Use the email address and password you registered with. Your session normally stays active for up to seven days. If it expires, sign in again when prompted.

2

Track daily habits

Open the Trackers section to record food, exercise, water, and weight activities. Your points, streak, and progress update after the information is saved.

3

Complete your lessons

Open Courses to read the weekly lesson, choose a day, and complete the weekly quiz on Day 7. Completed lessons help your research team understand participation.

4

Check the leaderboard

The Leaderboard shows research aliases, points, and earned badges. Your private name and email are not shown there.

5

Download your certificate

After completing the programme, open Profile to find your certificate. You can download it as a PDF, PNG, or JPEG using your registered full name.

6

Use the app offline

Previously loaded lessons can still be viewed without internet. Activities recorded offline are kept on your device and will try to sync when you reconnect.

Keep your account safe

Do not share your password. If you use a public or shared device, sign out when you finish. Contact the research team if your profile information is wrong or you lose access.
