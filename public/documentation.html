Dia Shield Documentation

This guide explains how to use your Dia Shield participant dashboard in clear, everyday language.

Participant guide

For participants

Using your health dashboard

1

Sign in

Use the email address and password you registered with. Your session normally stays active for up to seven days. If it expires, sign in again when prompted.

2

Track daily habits

Open the Trackers section and tap a button (Food Diary, Exercise, Water Log, or Weight) to open its form. Fill in the details and save — only entries you actually fill in are recorded, each with a timestamp, to your research log. Your points, streak, and progress update after the information is saved.

3

Complete your lessons

Open Courses to read the weekly lesson, choose a day, and complete the weekly quiz on Day 7. Completed lessons help your research team understand participation.

4

Check the leaderboard

The Leaderboard shows research aliases, points, and earned badges. Your private name and email are not shown there. It updates shortly after you save an entry — if your rank looks stale, open the Leaderboard tab again to refresh it.

5

How points are earned

Points are awarded only when you save an actual entry: each meal logged is worth points, each water count, each exercise session with a duration, and each weight reading. Marking a curriculum week complete and finishing quiz questions correctly also earn points. There is a daily cap per tracker type so the score reflects real activity rather than repeated taps. Your streak counts consecutive days with at least one saved entry.

6

Badges

Badges are awarded automatically when you reach milestones — your first healthy log, 8 glasses of water in a day, regular exercise, a 3- or 7-day streak, a correct quiz answer, a completed challenge, finishing week 1, and completing all 12 weeks. Each badge is awarded once; you cannot earn the same badge twice.

7

Download your certificate

After completing the programme, open Profile to find your certificate. You can download it as a PDF, PNG, or JPEG using your registered full name.

8

Use the app offline

Previously loaded lessons can still be viewed without internet. Activities recorded offline are kept on your device and will try to sync when you reconnect.

Week 5 activity self-monitoring

Week 5 ("Physical activity and diabetes prevention") includes a physical activity tracker on every day — Day 1 for Monday, Day 2 for Tuesday, and so on through Day 7 for Sunday. Each day's answers save independently, so you can fill in Monday on Monday and Tuesday on Tuesday without losing earlier entries. These personal entries are saved separately and are not used for clinical or inactivity alerts.

Keep your account safe

Do not share your password. If you use a public or shared device, sign out when you finish. Contact the research team if your profile information is wrong or you lose access.
