Research administration

Admin Dashboard Guide

This guide explains how the research team can review participation, record outcomes, follow up with users, and export study information.

For research administrators

Managing the study dashboard

1

Sign in securely

Open the admin dashboard and sign in with an authorised research-team email. Only approved administrator emails can view participant data.

2

Study overview

Use Overview to see enrolled participants, active users, retention, programme completion, quiz activity, and alerts needing attention.

3

Review participants

Participants shows each research alias, assigned week, modules opened and completed, quiz activity, challenges, last login, status, and earned badges.

4

Open a participant record

Select View beside a participant to see their profile, points, streak, progress, badges, assessments, and activity history.

5

Record research outcomes

Research Outcomes is where the team records Baseline and Endline weight, height, BMI, glucose, knowledge, perceptions, family history, activity, diet, smoking, and alcohol information.

6

Understand automatic alerts

Alerts highlight no activity for 14 days, missed modules, declining engagement, programme completion, and other follow-up needs.

7

Export study data

Use the export buttons to download participant engagement or research outcome data for approved reporting and analysis.

8

Delete an inactive account

A Delete button appears only for participants inactive for at least 14 days. Deletion is permanent and removes the account and associated study records. Confirm carefully before continuing.

Protect participant privacy

Use participant aliases when discussing records. Only authorised research-team members should access the admin dashboard, and exported files should be stored securely.
